# Journal

The Journal tab: imports, trades and spreads, fees, your stops and deletes, bars, trade features, the trade page, reviews, the review deck, calendar and stats, and the day notebook.

- **Journal** (header tab between Positions and Tools, setView('journal'), key j, state.journalView; ui/journal.js, #journalSection; journal step 2, docs/journal-step-2.md): scrolls as a page like Tools, centered 1100px column on the desktop shell.
  Signed out it asks you to sign in (the records live in your account).
  Signed in it reads the kept `schwab_records` (services/journal.js loadSavedRecords, newest first, limit 5000; row-level security returns only your rows) and shows: the kept line ('46 orders and 31 trades kept · Aug 17 to Oct 14', recordsSummary), Import and Export buttons in the card header, like other journals keep them apart: **Import** opens #journalImportSheet (SHEETS.journalImport, renderImportSheet) listing the sources, Schwab (connected ••last4, the last import, Import now → journalImport → captureNow; Connect Schwab when not connected) **thinkorswim statement** (futures; ui/journal-tos.js, core/tos-statement.js pure, state.tosImport): Schwab's API has no futures (they sit with Schwab's futures firm), so they come from thinkorswim desktop's Account Statement CSV (Monitor → Account Statement → Export to File). parseTosStatement reads the stacked sections: fills from Futures Statements' TRD rows ('BOT +1 /MNQZ26:XCME @29874.00', order number in Ref #, Misc Fees + Commissions as the fill's fee), opened or closed from Account Trade History's Pos Effect (matched on time, contract, side, qty, price; → BUY_TO_OPEN / SELL_TO_OPEN / BUY_TO_CLOSE / SELL_TO_CLOSE, so a close with nothing open is 'before your history'; no match = BUY / SELL_SHORT), the account as its last 4 only; stocks and options are left out (the Schwab import has them); a root FUTURES_CONTRACTS doesn't know, or a futures option, is skipped and named.
  Fill id tos:<order number>:<n> (its place among that order's fills), so an overlapping export adds nothing.
  The file's times have no zone: statementZone matches its Cash Balance stock fills (Ref # = Schwab's orderId) against the kept Schwab fills, else the device's zone; the preview names it and a select changes it.
  Preview (fills, contracts, days, fees, new vs already kept, skipped), then Save → kind 'statement_fill' rows in schwab_records (migration 20261101000000_statement_fills.sql widens the kind check; before the push the save says to run it), data = the fill. fillsFrom reads them, feesByOrder adds their fees, multiplierOf(assetType, symbol) gives a FUTURE its point value (futuresSpec: tick value ÷ tick size, MNQ $2, ES $50), stats count them under Futures, phone cards say cts.
  No charts: Tradier has no futures (tradeBarSymbols none, the trade page says so).
  Checked against a real statement: FIFO P&L matched its realized amounts to the cent.
  Then File upload (coming soon: Schwab's website export, Robinhood CSV, with a preview before saving); **Export** opens #journalExportSheet (SHEETS.journalExport, renderExportSheet): Schwab history (JSON) → downloadSchwabHistory when connected, Trades (CSV) coming soon.
  The card keeps the status line (importing, 'Last import …: N orders, M trades', or the last failure); the Import sheet redraws while open as a save starts or lands, and a fills table read out of the saved orders (core/journal-records.js orderFills / fillsFrom: each EXECUTION activity's legs matched to their order leg by legId, child orders walked, zero-quantity legs skipped; Symbol with option labels, Action in words, Qty, Price, Value, Time in New York time (marketTime, whatever the device zone)), newest 200 shown with the total counted.
  Opening the tab imports quietly when due; a save landing re-reads the records.

  **Trades** (slice 2; core/journal.js, pure): a Trades | Fills switch (state.journalTab, Trades by default) with side (All/Long/Short, state.journalSide) and symbol (state.journalSymbol, typed; the redraw keeps focus in the box) filters. buildTrades groups fills per account and symbol (an option's symbol is its contract) in time order, flat back to flat, first in first out: scale-ins add lots, a reducing fill matches the oldest lots (P&L = (exit − lot) × qty × multiplier, ×100 for options, signed by direction), a fill through zero closes the trade and opens the other way with the rest (a flip), unless its instruction only ever closes (CLOSING_ONLY: SELL, SELL_TO_CLOSE → long; BUY_TO_COVER, BUY_TO_CLOSE → short; a plain Sell can't open a short at Schwab).

  **Before your history**: a closing fill with nothing (or the other side) open, or the part of one beyond the position, closes something bought before Schwab's 60 days of orders: consecutive ones gather into one trade with status 'history' (key account|symbol|direction|before|first fill time, so a delete sticks), exits known, entry and P&L not; withRisk and withFees leave it alone, tradesSummary counts it apart (history) and never in the stats, and the row shows an amber 'before your history' tag, blanks and 'earlier'; its sheet (historySheet) has the facts and Delete only.

  **Merge and separate** (shapeTrades, after stops and fees; table trades; services/journal.js loadSavedTrades / insertSavedTrades / updateSavedTrade / deleteSavedTrades; state.journalSaved): a `trades` row (id, account, fill_ids) is a trade you shaped.
  A row claims every trade holding one of its fills (an open trade's later fills follow it), rows sharing a trade make one trade named by the oldest row ('trade:<id>', what trade_reviews keeps your stop and delete under), two or more trades on one symbol become kind 'merged' (quantities, P&L and fees added, weighted entry and exit, the stop and R found again over the span, your stop on the merged key first), option contracts kind 'spread' (spreadTrade, group 'yours'), and a row with one trade keeps it on its own (out of any multi-leg order's spread). mergeProblem refuses different accounts or underlyings, shares with options, one symbol on two sides, trades from before the history, deleted trades.
  UI: a checkbox in its own slot before each trade's name (toggleJournalPick, state.journalPicked, not on deleted rows; history rows can be ticked to delete, never merged); the pick bar under the table (sticky to the bottom, above the phone tab bar) says how many, Delete beside the count (deletePicked, away from Merge), why they can't be one, Merge into one trade and Clear. mergeTrades: a new row, or, when a picked trade has rows, its oldest takes the rest in (update) and the others are deleted, so its key stays; a new trade starts from the first part's own stop.
  Separate (the merged trade's sheet, and the spread sheet's Split into legs) deletes the rows, first giving each leg one order opened together a row of its own so they don't fold back.
  No trades table yet (journalSavedError): trades show as the app groups them, 'Merges unavailable', merging and combining say they need the db push.

  **Spreads** (shapeTrades): option legs fold into one trade (assetType 'SPREAD', legs, group 'auto' | 'yours', spreadKind debit | credit).
  'yours' = a trades row; legs one multi-leg order opened (shared entryOrderIds across two or more contracts) that no row claims are one spread ('auto', orderSpreads), keyed account|root|spread|first open time. trade_reviews.group_key (the first way spreads were combined) was dropped in the squash. spreadTrade: units = the smallest leg, entry and exit the net per unit, P&L/fees/exits summed, open while any leg is; risk = the debit (rBasis 'debit'), or for a two-leg same-expiry same-type credit vertical the width less the credit ('width'), else none; label '245/255C' or '230P/260C' by strike (spreadName), legWords '+3 245C −3 255C'.
  Row: a quiet tag with what it is (t.structure 'call debit' / 'put diagonal' / 'time spread', else 'debit spread' / 'credit spread'), the legs under the name, no stop link.
  The trade sheet: an option leg gets Combine into a spread (combineCandidates: other legs, same account and underlying, overlapping in time; combineTrade is a merge and the sheet stays on the spread); a spread shows its legs, Add a leg, Split into legs (splitSpread = Separate), and Delete (its own key).

  **Side = the bet on the stock** (t.bias via withBias; t.direction stays bought/sold for P&L and stops; the side filter uses bias): calls bought and puts sold are long, puts bought and calls sold short; a one-type spread goes by strikes, bought below what's sold (size-weighted) = long, above = short, so call debit / put credit verticals are long, put debit / call credit short, a put diagonal (longer-dated put bought, shorter-dated puts sold above it) and a poor man's covered call long; one strike over two expirations (time spread) neutral; mixed types the legs added up (an iron condor is neutral).
  A contract held alone and inside a spread is one leg trade (its contracts add up), so that spread shows the mixed sizes.

  **Expired options**: with journalTrades' `now` (the UI passes Date.now()), an option still open after expiryCloseMs (4pm New York on its expiry) closes there at $0 (expired: true, a quiet 'expired' tag; assumed worthless, unless Schwab has posted an assignment); identical-looking fills (a split order) stay separate since they're keyed by Schwab's execution ids.

  **Assignments and exercises** (assignmentFills in core/journal-records.js, part of fillsFrom): Schwab posts one as a TRADE transaction with no orderId (already captured), its stock leg at the strike, positionEffect OPENING (shares in) or CLOSING (shares out), settlementDate the day it happened (posted overnight; tradeDate is the next session); the option leg isn't in it.
  Each becomes fills at 4pm New York that day (Fill.via): the shares at the strike (BUY / SELL / SELL_SHORT / BUY_TO_COVER), and the open contracts on that underlying at that strike, expiring that day or later, closed at $0 (short first = 'assigned', else long = 'exercised'; shares in = a put sold or a call bought), so the premium stays the option's P&L and the shares cost the strike.
  No open contract in the history = the shares alone, 'delivered'. buildTrades takes those $0 fills; Trade.via tags the row ('assigned' / 'exercised' / 'delivered', quiet tag with a title, the trade page's meta line) and the Fills table says 'Buy (assigned)' (fillWords).
  The transaction's fees go to its shares (feesByOrder key tx:<activityId>; the option's tx:<activityId>:option has none rather than none yet).
  Checked against real assignments in the kept records.
  Trade key = account|symbol|direction|open time (stable: notes added later attach to it).
  Trades are computed every time; only the trades you shape are stored, as rows in `trades` (see Merge and separate).
  Initial stop (withRisk): the first stop order (stopOrdersFrom: STOP / STOP_LIMIT / TRAILING_*, children walked, a child without its own enteredTime takes its parent's) for that account and symbol whose instruction closes the direction, entered from 5 minutes before the first fill (STOP_LEAD_MS: a trigger's stop goes in with its entry) until the close, or attached to an order whose fill opened the trade (rootId in entryOrderIds, whenever it was entered: a resting entry's stop), on the loss side of the average entry (a stop past the entry protects profit, it isn't the risk); risk = |avg entry − stop| × entry quantity × multiplier; a long option with no stop risks its premium (rBasis 'premium'); R = P&L ÷ risk, closed trades only.

  **Fees**: a Before fees | After fees switch on the trades view (setJournalFees, `journal_fees` 'after' or 'before', a CLOUD_KEY so devices agree; cloudRehydrate redraws the Journal).
  Schwab's orders carry no fees; feesByOrder reads them from the kept TRADE transactions, which name their orderId and list each fee as a transfer item with a feeType (COMMISSION, SEC_FEE, TAF_FEE, OPT_REG_FEE, ...) at a negative cost; an order filled in pieces adds its transactions up. withFees gives each trade its orders' fees split by quantity (buildTrades' orderQty: a flip's order is shared by the trade it closes and the one it opens) and counts feesMissing (orders with no transaction yet).
  After fees, pnl, the win/loss result and R are net (grossPnl keeps the rest); the P&L cell says 'fees $X' or amber 'fees not in yet', its title the before-fees figure, and the summary 'after fees (fees $X)' and 'fees not in yet on N'.
  The transaction format is Schwab's documented one, not yet checked against a real import.
  Table: Symbol (open tag, the row tinted blue; no links on the row: the name is a button (journal-name, openTradeReview) and tapping anywhere else on the row opens the same sheet (openTradeRow, the tick box excepted), with a '›' after the name on touch screens; row actions follow the usual data-table pattern: open the row to edit, tick rows for bulk actions), P&L (so far on an open trade with exits), R (title says the stop and risk, or why there's none), Stop (the stop in use as a link to the trade sheet, 'yours' or 'moved to $X' under it; 'Set' with 'premium' under it for a long option without one), all right after Symbol so phones see them before scrolling sideways, then Side, Qty (open remainder under it), Entry, Exit, Hold (holdWords), Opened (ET); **P&L %** leads them (pnlPct / pnlBasis in core/journal.js, pure: P&L over what the closed part cost, avg entry × quantity closed × multiplier, a short's proceeds; a spread over its risk, the debit or a credit vertical's width less the credit, else the credit, closed only; 'so far' on an open trade's trims; none before anything closed or before your history; the title says what it's of), and the summary adds the average % beside the average R.

  **Columns** (the button right of the summary, table only, TRADE_COLS): chips hide or show every column but Symbol, one always stays, kept on this device as journal_hidden_cols (state.journalHiddenCols, so a column added later shows by default).
  Phone cards and the trade page's P&L stat show the % under the dollars; newest first by close (or open) time; summary line (tradesSummary): closed, won (win rate), P&L before fees, average R over the trades that have one, open, then 'N deleted · Show'.
  The how-it-works copy sits in a 'How trades are built' disclosure.

  **Phones** (PHONE_MQ, ≤600px, in lib/media.js; re-rendered when it flips) get a card per trade instead of the table (tradeCards, styled after a phone trade journal the owner liked): Open trades then Closed trades, each headed by its count and what it took ('Taken so far' from trims, 'Realized'); a card has the tick box, the name, pills (Open / Closed / Before your history, Long / Short / Neutral, the structure, expired, assigned, deleted), To review or Reviewed on the right, '20 shr · Oct 9 → Oct 13 · 4d 1h', the setups (amber 'No setup' when none), Entry / Exit / R / P&L, the stop in use as plain text (no control: it's changed on the trade page), and a solid blue Review button on every card but a deleted one; the whole card opens the trade page; the journal card goes flat so the trade cards sit on the page.
  The Journal's tabs are outlined pills (active blue).

  **Your word on a trade** (`trade_reviews`, one row per (user, trade key), stop numeric, deleted boolean (the delete mark), row-level security like every table; services/journal.js loadTradeReviews / saveTradeReview, read beside the records on every load into state.tradeReviews, upserted whole on (user_id, trade_key) so one field never clears the other).

  **Your stop** wins over Schwab's (withRisk's ownStop; stopSource 'yours' | 'schwab'), for an option's mental stop or a correction; on an option it's a price on the option itself (a stop on the underlying through the loss model isn't there yet); one past the entry (ownStopError: a price, on the loss side of the average entry) can't be saved and, if fills change under it, is ignored (ownStopIgnored).
  A moved Schwab stop never changes R: R counts from the first, and the trade's stopTrail (every closing stop in its window or attached to its entry, oldest first, past the entry included) gives movedTo, the last one's price.

  **Delete** (rare by design, so it sits one step away: in the trade sheet (deleteTrade, which closes it) or the pick bar for the ticked ones (deletePicked); no question first, the toast says 'Deleted AAPL.' with Undo (showToast's button, 8s; undoDeleteTrades puts back the rows kept in state.journalUndo); a refused save goes back with 'Couldn't save'; the trade's `deleted`): out of the list and the stats (tradesSummary only sees the rest).
  Schwab's records are never deleted: an import keeps bringing its orders (they'd come back anyway inside Schwab's 60-day window) and they stay kept and grouped, so the trades around it still add up; the mark is on the trade key, so every import and device keeps it deleted.
  A trade whose open time changes (an earlier fill imported later) is a new key and shows again.
  'Show' (state.journalShowDeleted) lists deleted ones dimmed with a deleted tag and Restore (restoreTrade, no question).
  The trade sheet (#tradeReviewSheet = SHEETS.tradeReview, state.tradeReviewKey; opened by the row, its name or the stop link): the trade's facts, the stop in use in words, Schwab's stops as chips (pickTradeStop fills the box; past-the-entry ones disabled), your stop box with a live risk / R preview (tradeStopPreview redraws only the preview and Save, so the cursor stays), Save stop, Use Schwab's stop instead / Go back to the premium / Remove your stop, and Delete this trade.
  A save shows at once, closes the sheet with a toast, and goes back with 'Couldn't save' in the sheet if Supabase refuses.
  If the reviews can't be read (the table not pushed yet: 'the database doesn't have the trade_reviews table yet'), the trades still show with Schwab's stops, an amber line says so, and saving is off so nothing is written over a row it couldn't read.

  **5-minute bars** (slice 3's groundwork: migration 20261019000000_bars_5m.sql, `bars_5m` one row per (user, Tradier symbol, New York day), bars packed as [time, o, h, l, c, v]; core/bars.js barsNeeded / barsByDay, services/bars.js saveTradeBars, ui/journal-bars.js): Tradier serves about 40 days of 5-minute bars, so the journal saves its trades' as it goes. barsNeeded: every weekday each trade was open (to its close, or the last finished day if still open; today only from 20:00 ET, when extended hours are over (lastDoneDay), since a day saved early would stay cut short), within BARS_KEEP_DAYS (35), for the stock, or for an option both the contract (spaces dropped) and its underlying, plus the LOOKBACK_DAYS (20) weekdays before the open for the stock / underlying (not the contract: comparisons like a volume run rate); history trades none; and, from maybeSaveBars (barsToKeep: MARKET_BASKET, SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA and the 11 sector SPDRs, plus every symbol a trade feature names in its needs, and the most lookback days any feature compares, at least LOOKBACK_DAYS; core/features featureBarNeeds), every weekday in the window for each basket symbol, traded or not (trade features, docs/journal-features.md). saveTradeBars reads the kept (symbol, day) since then, makes one call per symbol over its missing span through barSource (services/bar-source.js; Tradier timesales, 5min, 04:00–20:00, session_filter=all), and upserts one row per missing day with ignoreDuplicates and `source` = the provider's name (bars_5m.source, migration 20261029000000_bars_source.sql, default 'tradier'; before the push a refused source column retries without it); a day with no bars (a holiday) is kept empty so it's never asked again; a symbol Tradier refuses is skipped, named, and asked again next time. maybeSaveBars runs after the Journal's records load and after any import lands (journal open or not), needs a session and a Tradier key in the settings box, at most hourly per user (journal_bars, device-local: { user, at, saved, symbols, through, failed, error }); it runs quietly, the Import sheet says nothing about it (removed by request).
  The trade page reads them, and draws today (until it's saved) straight from Tradier, never saved.

  **Trade features** (docs/journal-features.md, part 2; core/features/, services/features.js, ui/journal-features.js; migration 20261030000000_trade_features.sql): what the data says about each trade at its entry, worked out by the app, never typed.
  A feature is one entry in a family file (market.js, volume.js, levels.js; define.js `feature(family, { key, name, desc, version, at: 'entry' | 'exit', needs, unit, values, bins, compute })`), listed in FEATURES (core/features/index.js); key permanent, name free to change, bump version (or the family's, for a shared helper) to restamp. needs: { intraday: ['$self' | '$underlying' | 'SPY'], lookback, daily, dailySessions (absent = back to the listing), session: 'regular' | 'all' }. compute(ctx) returns a number (raw, never a bucket), a word from values, null, or { value, note }.
  The context (featureContext, context.js) cuts the data at the moment: a 5-minute bar counts once it has ended, the bar the moment falls in only by its open; daily bars are the days before plus that day's open from 9:30; ctx.trade is only symbol, type, open time (and the close, at an exit); helpers worked out once per trade and moment (price, open, high so far, vwap(), volumeSoFar(), avgVolumeByTime(n), ema(n), emaWith(n, price), maxHigh(), prevClose, todayOpen, dailyBasis: the day's prices in the daily history's terms, the exact ratio of the 9:30 opens or the nearest split ratio within a day's 30% move, null when they can't line up, since Tradier's docs don't say whether daily history is split-adjusted).
  First batch: spy_vs_daily_ema8 / qqq_vs_daily_ema8 (price at entry, extended hours too, against the 60-session daily 8 EMA moved on by it; without that day's bars, the prior close vs the prior EMA, note 'prior close'), rvol_at_entry (regular-hours volume to the entry over the same time across the 20 sessions before, at least 10 kept; none before 9:35), entry_vs_vwap (a stock's first fill, an option's or spread's underlying price, against the session VWAP; none before 9:35), gap_at_open (daily open over the close before, from 9:30), dist_from_ath (against the highest prior daily high and that day's high so far, note 'high X on M/D/YY · history from YYYY').
  Stamps: `trade_features` one row per (user, trade key), `stamps` jsonb { key: { v: '<family version>.<version>', value, note } }, RLS like every table.
  Three states (computeFeature): a value; unknown for good (value null with why, stamped, never retried until the version changes); waiting (data that could still come: a day inside BARS_KEEP_DAYS the saver hasn't kept, today's bars, daily bars not read: not stamped, state.featureWaits says what for). maybeStampFeatures runs after the Journal loads (once maybeSaveBars settles, with the trades' keys as the Journal has them) and after an import (stampAfterImport, journal closed: re-reads the records), one run at a time: featuresDue (stampable: not deleted, not before your history; a feature missing or with another v) → per chunk of 40 trades featureDataNeeds (each symbol's moment day and lookback days, read from bars_5m; daily bars from barSource.daily once a run per symbol, back to 1960 for the listing, needing the Tradier key) → stampTrade (a removed feature's stamps dropped from rows it writes) → upsert.
  Only waiting ones left from the last run: tried again at most every 15 minutes (FEATURES_RETRY_MS).
  Quiet line under the Journal's status (#journalFeaturesLine, 'Stamping 6 features on 412 trades…', then '… 120 of 412' as chunks land, or a failed run).

  **Features** tab (between Model books and Fills, tabs only): per family every feature with its name, what it measures, what it reads (featureNeedsWords), version, and how far it's stamped (featureCoverage: 'N of M', unknown with the commonest reason, to come).
  The trade page has two tabs under its stats (state.journalTrade.tab, setJournalTradeTab; Review by default, every trade opens on it): **Review** (the review section, the fills, the stop orders) and **Features** (count of values on the tab; #jtFeatures, tradeFeaturesHtml, redrawn in place as a run lands): every feature by family as a row (name, value, its note or what it waits for) that taps open (<details>) to what it measures, what it reads, its version and key; Expand all / Collapse all (toggleTradeFeatures, state.journalTrade.featuresOpen).
  Before the migration is pushed (state.featuresError) nothing is worked out, the Features tab and trade page say it needs the db push, and the rest of the Journal works. tests/features.test.mjs checks every feature for look-ahead (everything after the moment changed, or cut off, gives the same answer at many moments) and its contract (key, version, family, output, and reads only what needs declares).
  Conditions filter and the Stats explorer are part 3.

  **Trade page** (slice 3: ui/journal-trade.js, #journalTradeSection, state.journalTrade; core/trade-page.js pure): a row opens it (openTradeRow → openJournalTrade; the list hides, syncJournalPage, views.js shows one or the other), ← Trades goes back, Edit opens the trade sheet (stop, separate, delete).
  Header: the name, side, size, entry → exit (the size-weighted average, 'avg' after it, when there were several exits; an open trade with trims says '60 sold at $0.71 avg · 40 open' instead; exitParts / exitLineLabel in core/trade-page.js, the chart's line reads 'avg exit 0.71 · 60 of 100').
  Charts (static markup, initJournalTradeEvents at boot, candles.js painting and gestures; paintCandles gained `marks`: buys an up triangle under the bar in blue, sells a down one over it in amber): **5 min** from the saved bars (loadTradeBars on the hold's weekdays; missing days Tradier still has are saved first through saveTradeBars, then re-read), the stock's or an option contract's own (a spread shows its underlying), regular hours unless a fill was outside them (holdChartBars), entry / exit / stop lines on the trade's own chart, the session VWAP in teal (sessionVwap: typical price × volume, starting over each New York day, over the bars shown, so from 4:00 when the extended hours are; broken between days; blue is the entry line and the buys; in the legend beside the 8 EMA; none on the daily); **Daily** from Tradier (dailyHistory of the stock or the option's underlying), framed 40 sessions before the open through 10 after the close (dailyWindow). fillMarks puts each fill on the bar it happened in (barStart, nyStamp: New York time) or its day.
  Stats: P&L, R, MFE and MAE (excursions: best high / worst low against the average entry over the bars from the open's bar through the close's, shorts the other way, on the whole entry × multiplier, R on the risk) and exit efficiency (P&L ÷ MFE, winners only, at most 100%), hold; spreads and before-history trades get no excursions.
  Then the fills (Time ET, action, qty, price) and the stop orders over time (entered, stop with 'first' on Schwab's initial one, status).
  Missing bars say why (no key, older than Tradier keeps, today's session not saved yet).

  **Review** (step 3, slice 1; docs/journal-step-3.md; core/journal-labels.js pure, services/journal-review.js, ui/journal-review.js; migration 20261020000000_trade_review_labels.sql): a Review section on the trade page, under the stats.
  Defaults live in code (DEFAULTS: market, sector, level, action, pattern, tactic, tag, mistake, each [key, name]); `library_items` holds your own items (`mine:<uuid>`) and rows that rename or hide a default (default_key) or merge one item into another (merged_into, an alias: labels on it resolve to the kept one); buildLibrary layers them (choices, nameOf, resolve).
  Labels are `trade_labels` rows (trade key, kind sector | setup | tactic | tag | mistake, value an item key, set_by you | app); sector takes one (SINGLE), the rest any number (a trade can have several entry tactics); **My setups**: a setup is a library item of kind 'setup' (migration 20261021000000_setups_and_links.sql allows it), saved once and picked from then on: an action (Bounce, Undercut & rally, Rejection) at one or more levels, or a pattern, kept in its settings ({ action, levels } or { pattern }; DEFAULT_SETUP_PARTS for the nine starting ones: 8/21 EMA and 50 SMA bounce, 21 EMA and 50 SMA undercut & rally, 21 EMA rejection, base breakout, flag breakout, earnings gap); a setup whose name is its parts' in the defaults' words ('8 EMA bounce') was never typed, so it follows them renamed (the 8 EMA level renamed 9 EMA makes it '9 EMA bounce'; a typed name stays).
  The row shows them as chips (lit ones first, then by use: setupsByUse), a title with what each is made of (partsWords).
  '+ New setup' (state.setupDraft) asks What happened? / At what? (several) / Or a pattern / Name (setupName fills it): as you pick, the chips narrow to the setups that fit (setupFits) so you tap one you have; an exact match (sameParts) says 'Already in My setups: tap it' (useSetupDraftMatch) instead of Save; otherwise 'Save to my setups and add' makes it (a same-name or same-parts one is reused).
  Edit choices lists My setups first, with what each is made of.

  **Links on a tag**: trade_labels.links (jsonb array); under a lit Option flow tag (or any tag with links) a box takes pasted links (cleanLink: https only, 500 chars, ten at most, each once) shown as chips named for the site (linkLabel: Unusual Whales, Bullflow, else the host) that open in a new tab (rel noopener noreferrer); saveLabelLinks upserts the label's links.
  Older setup values ('sma50+bounce', 'pattern:x') still name themselves.
