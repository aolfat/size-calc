# Schwab trading

The Schwab login and worker, Trade at Schwab, the banner, account size from Schwab, raw capture into the journal, and the history download.

- **Schwab trading** (optional; new trades are shares only, risk sizing only; held positions get a stop at your price or at breakeven and a market or limit close, all or part, from Positions): the shares card's **Trade at Schwab** button (#sharesTrade, shown only when connected, in shares mode with risk sizing, for stock/ETF quotes; disabled without a valid stop) refreshes the quote, then opens the review sheet (#tradeSheet, SHEETS.trade). buildTradeTicket freezes one ticket from sharesPlan() (the same math renderShares shows): qty, the stop snapped to Schwab's tick toward the entry (stopTick: cents at $1+, 4 decimals below), and the exact payload (sharesStopOrder: a DAY entry at market, or a limit, a stop or a stop limit, orderStrategyType TRIGGER, child STOP for the same shares; long = BUY → SELL, short = SELL_SHORT → BUY_TO_COVER).
  **Entry order** (the sheet's first row): Market | Limit | Stop | Stop limit (t.entryType; sharesStopOrder takes entryType, entryStop, entryLimit: a STOP or STOP_LIMIT parent with stopPrice at the planned entry on priceTick, a stop limit's price its limit, the same TRIGGER child stop).
  **Limit** is a LIMIT parent with price entryLimit (the ticket's limitPrice, its own field so a stop limit's default stays put); a review never opens on it, and picking it doesn't change schwab_entry_type.
  The limit starts at the planned entry on Schwab's tick and is typed in the sheet (#tradeLimit; typing redraws only #tradeDetail and the Place label); the share count stays as the card sized it.
  Risk at stop for a limit is at the limit, the worst it can fill, with the plan after it; the over-plan warning reads it there, and limitEntryError (no price, or a limit at or through the protective stop) holds the order back like a stop limit's limitError.
  A limit's protective stop is checked against the market, as for Market, since it can fill right away (sharesTicketError only checks stop entries against the entry).
  The hint says whether it likely fills right away (at or through the ask / bid) or waits, and that an unfilled DAY entry ends at the close with its stop never placed.
  Once sent, a limit Schwab shows still working with nothing filled gets **Change limit** beside ↻ Status, which opens Positions' Edit for that entry (docs/positions.md): it cancels the entry and places it again with the new price and the same stop.
  A planned entry past the market (over the ask on a long, under the bid on a short: a breakout, like the opening-range chips set) opens as the stop entry you picked last (schwab_entry_type, device-local, default Stop limit); anything else opens at Market.
  The stop types grey out with the reason when the entry isn't past the market (stopEntryError: it would fill right away).
  Stop limit shows a Limit box, first 0.2% past the stop (at least a tick; defaultEntryLimit); typing it re-plans and redraws only #tradeDetail, and a limit under a buy's stop (over a short's) shows inline and holds the order back (entryLimitError, t.limitError).
  With a stop entry, risk is counted from the entry's stop, a stop limit also says what it risks filled at its limit (in the risk row, not a warning: a default limit on a tight range is always a bit over), and the protective stop is checked against the entry instead of today's bid (sharesTicketError with entryType).
  Re-checked at Place like the rest: a price that broke through the entry since the review sends nothing.
  The stop lasts Today or Until canceled (schwab_stop_duration, default GOOD_TILL_CANCEL); the entry is always DAY. sharesTicketError blocks a stop already through the bid (long) or ask (short), sub-one-share sizes, and non-stock types.
  Warnings: outside 9:30–16:00 ET weekdays (no holiday table), risk at the current ask/bid >10% over the plan, a same-symbol order in the last 2 minutes.
  The review only opens on a fresh quote for the loaded symbol (refreshQuote answers true/false; a failed refresh shows an error, no ticket).
  Place refreshes the quote again and re-checks the frozen order against it: a new error or warning blocks the send and shows the updated ticket, and a second Place sends it.
  Then it sends the frozen payload ONCE (ticket.sent) to the account the ticket was built for, and nothing retries it; an error before any request (token refresh, account switch) says nothing was sent and arms no repeat warning; a network failure says to check Schwab before retrying.
  After a 201 the order id comes from Location and one status read reports fill and stop status (↻ Status re-reads).
  Login: Settings → Schwab trading takes the worker URL, Log in opens `<worker>/login?state=…`, the user pastes the callback address (https://127.0.0.1 style), or (the recommended setup, and how the owner's app is configured) the app finishes by itself when its own URL is the callback (initSchwab reads ?code, requires the stored state, strips the query).
  The worker reports its callback at `GET /callback` (CORS like the rest); the app asks on load and when the worker URL changes (600ms debounce), caches it per worker URL as `schwab_callback` (device-local, kept on logout), and hides the paste row (#schwabPaste) when callbackIsHere (same origin and path, trailing slash and index.html aside); a worker that can't say, or a 127.0.0.1 callback, keeps the paste box.
  The worker URL box folds to one line ('Worker: <host> · Change', #schwabProxyLine) once the URL is set and known to work (it answered /callback, or a Schwab login is live) and the box isn't focused; Change (editSchwabProxy) reopens it until the box loses focus (schwabProxyDone, state.schwabProxyEditing).
  Empty or unanswered keeps the box, since that's where setup starts.
  Tokens (`schwab_tokens`, bound to the Google user or signed-out session, worker URL and a unique connection id: access ~30 min, refreshed with a 2-min margin through one shared in-flight refresh; refresh ends 7 days after login), accounts (hash + last 4 only), and the chosen account are device-local: not CLOUD_KEYS, so never in the account; each device logs in on its own.

  **Banner** (#schwabBanner under the header, every view; updateSchwabBanner in ui/trade.js from updateSchwabUi and visibilitychange, schwabStanding in services/schwab.js): once a worker URL is set and boot is done (state.schwabBannerReady, after initSchwab), says Schwab isn't connected on this device, the login ended, no account is loaded, or (amber) the login ends within 24h; quiet while a live login waits for its Google session; Log in to Schwab opens Settings and starts the login; × hides it until the app is opened again (state.schwabBannerHidden).
  The worker URL (`schwab_proxy`) is the one Schwab setting that syncs, so a new device only needs Log in.
  Since the login goes through whoever runs that worker, a URL the account changes (cloud applySynced) is marked `schwab_proxy_confirm` (device-local): the first Log in press names the new host and asks, the second uses it, and finishSchwab refuses a code meanwhile; typing the URL on the device clears it.
  The login attempt's state is used once: a wrong state or a refused code deletes it.
  Restoring the Google session (startSession) clears a Schwab login that isn't this user's or has ended, but keeps the attempt (schwabDisconnect({ keepAttempt: true })): on the callback page it runs first, and clearing the state there made every login say 'from another attempt'. main.js strips a Schwab ?code from the address at boot (stripSchwabCallback), before the Google session loads.
  `worker/schwab/` is a Cloudflare Worker: `/login` redirect, `/token` and `/refresh` with the app secret (Basic auth, only access/refresh/expires_in returned), `/trader/v1/*` relay passing the bearer through and exposing Location; browser calls only from ALLOWED_ORIGINS; it stores nothing.
  It also relays `GET /marketdata/v1/*` the same way, read only, for futures tickers' quotes and bars (docs/calculator.md, Futures in Stocks & futures); a worker deployed before that answers 404 there until it is redeployed, and the Schwab app needs Market Data Production.
  Positions reads and trades through the same login (see Positions).

  **Account size from Schwab** (ui/account-size.js): while logged in, the selected account's liquidation value (rounded to dollars) becomes the account size once per New York day, on the day's first Schwab read: boot, window focus / visibilitychange, a Positions refresh (which reuses its own read, no extra call; dailyAccountSize skips while Positions is open), a login or account switch (a different account is due right away).
  It goes through the normal account-size path, so calc_account, signed in, syncs to the account (a CLOUD_KEY); the risk % holds and the max loss follows, unless a $ chip is lit and no % chip is (riskFollowsDollars), which keeps the dollars.
  A toast says what changed.
  A number typed later holds until the next day.
  The risk sheet's #acctSourceRow (shown only while connected) switches From Schwab daily / Typed in (schwab_acct_source, kept on logout; back on updates now); the day's marker schwab_acct_size {day, hash, value, at} is device-local and goes with the login.
  A failed or zero read changes nothing and quietly retries on the next focus.

  **Raw capture** (journal step 2, slice 1a: services/capture.js, ui/schwab-capture.js, table schwab_records): every Schwab order (last 59 days) and TRADE transaction (a year back the first time, 59 days if Schwab refuses, then from a week before the last save) kept whole in Supabase's `schwab_records`, one row per order (children inside) and per transaction, keyed (user, kind, Schwab id), account = last 4, entered_at ISO, data = Schwab's JSON with account numbers masked (captureRows in core/schwab-history.js).
  Imports are deltas: every row carries `fingerprint` (SHA-256 hex of stableJson(data), so key order never counts), the import reads the kept (record_id, fingerprint) for the ids it just read (batches of 200), and planDelta sends only new orders, orders whose fingerprint changed (status, fills), and new trades; a trade (transaction) never changes once posted, so it goes in with ignoreDuplicates (on conflict do nothing); a kept row with no fingerprint (saved before them) is rewritten to fill one in and counted as kept.
  The result carries newOrders / updatedOrders / newTrades / kept, and importWords says it: the toast ('3 new trades, 2 orders updated, 71 already kept.' or 'Nothing new, 77 already kept.') and the Journal's status line (an older device's totals-only record still shows its totals).
  Imports pin the Google user, broker account, and connection across all asynchronous reads and writes, and cancel if any changes.
  Runs quietly when both logins are in place (Google session + Schwab account): on sign-in, on Schwab connect, on focus, on opening the Journal, at most every 6 hours per user and account (captureDue; last success device-local as `schwab_capture`); the Journal's Import (captureNow) runs it any time. ui/schwab-capture.js tells listeners (onCaptureChange) when a save starts or lands; a quiet failure shows on the Journal's status line, not as a toast.

  **Download Schwab history** (journal step 0, temporary: ui/schwab-history.js, #schwabHistoryBtn in the Journal's Export sheet, shown when Schwab is connected): one JSON file with positions, the last 59 days of orders, and TRADE transactions (schwabTransactions) over 30/60/90/180/365 days, each read's answer or error kept, a summary of rows and date span per read, every accountNumber / accountId / hashValue masked to its last 4 (core/schwab-history.js scrubAccounts, pure), saved through effects.downloadText; for building the importer against real data, removed once it lands (docs/journal-step-2.md).
  No Schwab sandbox exists for individual developers: every order is real
