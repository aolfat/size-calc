# Positions

The Positions tab: rows, spreads, optional columns and R, position trades (stop, breakeven stop, close, spread close), working orders, cancel, edit, and the position chart with profit targets.

- **Positions** (Schwab, read-only): a top-level header tab (Positions, count badge #posCount = rows in the last read, key v), not part of the instrument switcher. positionsView is a display state, NOT a mode — currentMode stays shares/options underneath (tradeCtx etc. unaffected); selecting it hides all sizing surfaces (including the ticket card) and shows #positionsSection only: a live table of the account picked in Settings → Schwab trading, straight from Schwab (no Tradier key needed).
  Two reads per refresh: `GET /accounts/{hash}?fields=positions` (schwabPositions) and the last 59 days of orders (schwabRecentOrders; Schwab's window is 60).
  Columns: Symbol (options as 'AAPL 6/20/26 245C' from Schwab's padded OCC symbol), Qty (shorts negative), Avg cost, Price (market value / qty × multiplier, Schwab's own mark), Market value, % of acct (of liquidation value, not the risk strip's account size), Stop, Risk @ stop; total row sums value and loss-only risk.

  **Spreads** (positionSpreads in core/positions.js, pure; p.spreads beside p.rows, which keep every leg so the chart, sim and trade sheets still find them by symbol): Schwab lists each leg on its own, so legs fold back into one row: legs a filled (or partly filled) order opened together (two or more contracts with BUY_TO_OPEN / SELL_TO_OPEN, newest order first, still held on the same sides), else an underlying whose only ungrouped options are one bought and one sold of the same type and size (opened before the 59 days, or legged in); anything else stays single.
  The row (spreadRow): label like the Journal's ('RBRK 8/28/26 105/115C', 'RBRK time spread' across expiries), a quiet structure tag (call debit, put credit, time spread, call diagonal, debit spread), the legs ('+3 105C −3 115C'), Qty in spreads (credit negative, like any short), Avg cost the net debit or credit per spread, Price the net mark, value / P/L / Today added up (P/L % of the net), Risk @ stop its max loss: a debit whose bought leg covers the sold one (one each, same type and size; calls bought at or below, puts at or above) risks the debit, a credit vertical width less the credit, ratios / mixed types / more legs none; the total risk counts that instead of the legs' stops; R = P/L ÷ max loss (spreadR).
  Legs (togglePosLegs, state.posLegsOpen) shows the legs under it as indented rows with their own buttons; the tab count counts a spread once.

  **Close** on the spread row (ui/spread-close.js, #spreadCloseSheet = SHEETS.spreadClose, state.spreadTicket) closes or trims it as ONE multi-leg order (spreadClosePlan in core/positions.js, pure): every bought leg SELL_TO_CLOSE and sold leg BUY_TO_CLOSE in the legs' ratio (legs not in a whole ratio of the smallest are refused: close them under Legs), Net limit (the default, at the mark on the option grid; a debit spread closes NET_CREDIT, a credit spread NET_DEBIT; Today / Until canceled) or Market (DAY); quantity in spreads with 25% / 50% / 75% / All chips of what's held now (a chip stays a portion across the re-read).
  All cancels every order in play that closes a leg first (working spread closes, spreadClosers; then the legs' targets; then their stops); a trim leaves them and must fit in what they leave free on every leg.
  Warned: outside regular hours, a limit at or through the mark (likely fills right away), market spreads filling each leg at its own market.
  Same send rules as the position trades: re-read and re-planned at send (different steps or a new warning send nothing), sent once, account pinned, confirmed when every leg is smaller by its share or Schwab lists the new net limit, unconfirmed blocks that spread for 2 minutes (state.posLast, keyed by the spread).

  **Optional columns** (Columns button → chips, off by default, picks kept on this device as positions_cols, between Market value and % of acct): P/L and P/L % from your average cost (null without one; % of cost), Today and Today % from Schwab's currentDayProfitLoss (% of the value before today's move); the total row sums them over the rows that have them.

  **R** (also optional; positionR in core/positions.js, pure): open P/L ÷ the risk at entry.
  The risk is the Journal's open trade on the position (same account last 4, contract and direction, not deleted, not inside a spread, and holding the same quantity as Schwab shows): its first stop, your own stop, or a long option's premium, so trailing the stop up never shrinks it ('first stop $226.00' under the figure; '+2.58R with trims' when its exits already took some, before fees like Schwab's P/L).
  Otherwise (signed out, not imported yet, a quantity the Journal hasn't caught up to: the title says so) it's the nearest working stop's distance from your average cost ('stop now $40.00'), or a long option's premium with no stop; a stop at or past breakeven, no stop, or no cost basis is a dash with the reason in its title.
  No total.
  With the column on and signed in, Positions reads the kept records, your stops and merges itself (readPositionJournal: once, '…' in the cells meanwhile, state.posJournalBusy; a failure waits for a Refresh, state.posJournalError) and again when an import lands while it's open; the Journal's trades are built once per change of records, stops, merges or fee choice (state.posRTrades).
  A reload on Positions while signed in reads the account once the stored session is back (startSession: the Schwab login is bound to the Google user, so it doesn't count before).

  **Hide** beside the account value masks it and cash ('Account value hidden', positions_hide_value, device-local; the daily account-size toast still names it).

  **Simulate** on option rows (ui/position-sim.js, in the Chart button's slot): one Tradier quotes call (underlying + the contract with greeks, Schwab's padded OCC symbol with the spaces taken out; needs a key, requireKey) opens the Simulate returns modal at your average cost and size; a sold option measures P/L the other way, as % of the premium received (puts too: on assignment notional a held put barely moves), with no max-loss line.
  Positions have no underlying stop, so the sim swaps stop / 1R / 2R for −1σ / +1σ chips (em = spot × IV × √years to expiry) and sizes its slider from that move, not the calculator ticker's ADR core/positions.js (pure, tested): restingOrders and positionRows walk orders and their childOrderStrategies (so the stop half of a filled TRIGGER counts) for resting single-leg STOP/STOP_LIMIT orders whose instruction closes the position (SELL/SELL_TO_CLOSE on longs; BUY_TO_COVER/BUY_TO_CLOSE/BUY on shorts; AWAITING_PARENT_ORDER excluded).
  Nearest stop leads; each fills against the shares the ones before it left.
  Risk = (avg − stop) × qty × multiplier from YOUR average cost (side-aware), negative = green 'locked'; 'covers X of Y' when stops cover part; blank without a stop or cost basis.
  A failed orders read still shows positions ('Stops unavailable'); a failed refresh keeps the last table with the error.
  Refresh: on open, ↻ Refresh button (and r), every 30s while the tab is open and the page visible (setTimeout chain, never overlapping; visibilitychange pauses and refreshes on return). positionsRequest drops late answers; login, logout and account switch (positionsAccountChanged) clear the old account's table.
  Without a login: a Connect Schwab card that opens Settings.

  **Phone cards** (≤600px, PHONE_MQ, checked at each render and again when the width crosses it): a card per position instead of the sideways table, no Columns.
  Over them, three tiles: Account (the liquidation value in whole dollars, 'Hidden' with Hide), Invested (the market values added up) and Open P/L; the head line keeps cash, Hide, the time and Refresh.
  Each card: the symbol (an option's strike before its expiry, '335C 6/20/26', so the strike survives the ellipsis) and its P/L top right, then 'avg → price · qty sh' (ct for options) with P/L % and R beside it.
  R is the R column's (positionR), so on a phone the Journal is read when signed in, as when the column is on; while that read is out the card leaves R off.
  The ladder under it puts the nearest stop (amber tick), your average cost (grey tick) and the price (dot) on one line, padded at both ends, the stretch from cost to price red or green with the P/L; a long option with no stop starts the line at zero, its premium being the risk.
  The line under the ladder: 'Stop $19.50' (+N more, covers X of Y), 'At breakeven' or 'Stops past breakeven · +$X locked' in green, 'No stop · max loss is premium' on a long option, 'No stop' in amber otherwise.
  A tap on the card (togglePosCard, state.posCardsOpen, not kept) opens Mkt val, Stop and Risk @ stop and the row's buttons (Chart, Simulate, Stop, BE stop, Close in red).
  A spread's card shows its net cost → net mark in spreads, its structure and max loss, a ladder from zero for a debit spread (none for a credit), and opens to Mkt val, Max loss, the legs and Legs / Close; Legs lists each leg as its own card under it.
  Working orders stay a table that scrolls sideways.

  **Phone layout** (Settings → Positions: Cards | Table; setPosLayout, state.posLayout, positions_layout, this device only, Cards by default): Table is the old sideways table rebuilt for a thumb.
  The account value sits large over it with the open P/L under it; the symbol column stays pinned with the size ('40 sh'), an option's contract ('55C 12/18/26') or 'stop at breakeven' (green) under the symbol; then Price, P/L, P/L %, Mkt val, Qty, Avg, Stop, Risk @ stop and R, a swipe away, and a Total row (P/L, P/L %, value, the loss-only risk).
  A tap on a row (openPosActions, state.posActions) opens #posActionsSheet (SHEETS.posActions): the P/L, cost → price, the cards' stop ladder and stop line, Mkt val / Stop / Risk @ stop, and the actions two by two with Close across the bottom: Chart, Simulate on options, Set stop or Edit stop, Move stop to breakeven (gone once every share is at or past it), Close; a spread gets Show legs / Hide legs and Close spread.
  The buttons go through posSheetAct: Chart, Simulate and the legs show on the page, so the sheet closes first; Stop, breakeven, Close and the spread close open their own review sheets.
  The sheet redraws with each 30s read, and says so when the position is gone.

  **% only** (Settings → Positions: $ and % | % only; setPosPnl, state.posPnlPct, positions_pnl_pct, this device only): hides the dollar P/L of open positions everywhere in Positions.
  The cards and the actions sheet lead with the P/L % (R alone beside cost → price), the Open P/L tile and the table's open P/L line show the percent, the phone table drops its P/L column, and the desktop table drops the P/L and Today columns and their chips from Columns (the picks are kept for when dollars come back).
  Market value, cash, the account value and Risk @ stop stay in dollars; the account value has its own Hide.

  **Position trades** (ui/position-trade.js, #posTradeSheet = SHEETS.posTrade): stock, ETF/closed-end fund (COLLECTIVE_INVESTMENT) and option rows (row.tradeAs) get **Stop**, **BE stop** and **Close** buttons under the symbol, in the pinned column; a position whose every share already has a stop at or past breakeven (long: stop ≥ be; short: ≤ be) reads 'stop at breakeven' or 'stops past breakeven' instead.
  Both re-read Schwab first, then freeze a ticket from pure plans in core/positions.js. breakevenPlan NEVER loosens a stop: stops (and pairs) already at or past breakeven are kept as they are and count as covering their shares (breakevenCovered / atBreakeven); only the remaining shares get a breakeven stop at row.be (stocks stopTick toward the market, options optionStopTick: $0.05 under $3, $0.10 from $3, the grid every option class accepts, so up to a dime better than cost) without ever asking Schwab to sell more than you hold, which it refuses through the API ('may result in an oversold/overbought position').
  The shares outside any limit target (rest) get one plain stop.
  When another order already closes part of it (closersInTheWay: limit targets, pair legs, anything else waiting), the sheet asks first and sends nothing until answered (setPositionBeMode, t.choose / t.mode, nothing preselected; an option that can't work is greyed out with its reason): **Stop the rest** (breakevenPlan(..., { limits: 'keep' }): the limits stay as they are and their shares (plan.bare) keep no stop; greyed out when a limit holds the whole position), **Pair with the limit** ({ limits: 'pair' }, the default: the shares a limit holds get a stop paired with that limit as an OCO, one cancels the other), or **Cancel the limit** ({ limits: 'cancel' }: every other closing order is cancelled, limits and pairs first, then the extra stops, and the nearest stop is replaced to cover the whole position; also the way out when a market or other order blocks the first two).
  Positions with nothing in the way get no question.
  Steps: cancel the limits being paired (and pairs whose stop isn't at breakeven), cancel the extra plain stops farthest first, replace (or place) the nearest plain stop for the rest, then place each limit again as OCO(limit, BE stop); paired shares have no stop for a moment and the sheet says so.
  A paired limit takes the stop's duration and regular hours (warned when that changes it).
  Blocked when the price is through the stop, without a cost basis, for fractional shares, when another kind of order (market, etc.) is waiting to close part of it, when the closing orders already exceed what you hold, or when every share already has a breakeven stop.

  **Stop** (t.kind 'stop', stopPlan in core/positions.js, pure) is the breakeven stop at a price you type: the same never-loosen rules and the same Stop the rest / Pair with the limit / Cancel the limit question (breakevenPlan and stopPlan share stopsAt), with stops at or tighter than your price (long: at or above; short: at or below) kept and counted as covering their shares.
  The price snaps to priceTick (options optionPriceTick) and starts at the nearest stop when some shares have none, else empty; a **Breakeven** chip fills row.be when the price is past it.
  Typing re-plans and redraws only #posTradeDetail (the cover question sits there too, since its choices change with the price); no price, a stop at or through the mark, or every share already at or tighter than it shows inline and disables the send (moving a stop looser is Edit under Working orders).
  Risk at stop: what the stops leave from your average cost afterwards, the kept ones at their own prices (red with % of the account value, green 'locked' past cost).
  It works without a cost basis (no breakeven, so no chip and no risk figure).
  The send, the re-read and the confirmation are the breakeven stop's.
  A stop that loses more than the risk strip's max loss per trade is warned ('At the stop this loses $360.00, more than your max loss per trade, $300.00'; maxLossPerTrade in ui/risk.js, none in allocation sizing), measured like Risk at stop (stopLocked in core/positions.js).

  **Close** is a broker-style ticket (t.close: Market | Limit, quantity with 10% to 90% (25% and 75% too) and All chips of the original size (All = everything held), default all at market, at least 1 (rounded down), a chip that's more than you hold ('320 shares, more than the 300 left') or than other limits leave free greyed out with why, the lit chip the one pressed (close.portion, typing clears it); a limit starts at Schwab's mark, stocks on priceTick, options on optionPriceTick, the nearest $0.05 / $0.10 step, and lasts schwab_stop_duration).
  **The original size** (t.original = positionOriginal in ui/positions.js, originalSize in core/journal.js, pure): the most held at once since the position was last flat, so 'bought 100, sold 50, bought 50' is 100, not the 150 bought.
  It comes from the Journal's own fill reader and trade grouping (fillsFrom, buildTrades: only real fills, so Schwab's canceled orders listed as $0 executions don't count; a new trade after going flat; assignments from Schwab's transactions; options past expiry closed), over the Journal's kept records (signed in, so older than Schwab's 59 days) plus the orders this read got (an order in both is taken from the read, the newer; state.posFills memoizes the fills).
  Your Journal merges and deletes don't count: it's what the fills hold.
  It's trusted only when those fills leave the position holding what Schwab holds now; otherwise (opened before the history, shares in by transfer or a split, signed out with older fills) the chips are of what's held and the line under them says why ("% of what's held: the original size is unknown. The fills in … leave 50 shares open, not the 150 held.").
  Known and bigger than held, the line reads '% of your original 400 shares, the most held since you were last flat. 300 left (75%).'; equal to held (no trims yet), no line.
  Opening Close reads the Journal first when signed in and not read yet (readPositionJournalForClose, alongside the Positions re-read), so the chips start on the right base.
  A trim's detail shows **Left after** ('200 shares · This closes 25% of your original 400 shares, leaving 50%.'), typing included.
  The spread close keeps its 25 / 50 / 75 / All of what's held.
  **Works in** (stocks and ETFs, a limit close and a target; setPositionSession, ticket.session, not remembered): Regular (NORMAL, the default), Pre (AM, 7:00 to 9:25), After (PM, 16:05 to 20:00) or Ext (SEAMLESS, all three), New York time.
  Only the limit takes the session: the stop paired with it stays NORMAL, since Schwab's stops trigger in regular hours only, and the review warns that nothing stops the shares outside them.
  The steps and the send button name the session ('limit $41.10 (after hours)'), and the hours warning follows it (inOrderSession in core/orders.js).
  Market orders and options stay in regular hours; overnight (thinkorswim's EXTO) isn't offered, since Schwab's API documents no session for it.
  **Limit in R** (#posCloseR, R chips 1R 2R 3R 5R in #posCloseRChips, close.r): the chart's Target in R on the close ticket, so 'sell half at 3R' is the 50% chip and the 3R chip; a chip sets the limit that many R from your cost (rTargetPrice, the same 1R as the chart: rUnit / positionRUnit), typing R moves the price, and typing a price clears the R and the box shows the price in R.
  The note under it says what 1R is and where it's from; no risk to count from greys the box and chips out with why.
  The Estimate counts R too, at the mark for a market close and at the limit for a limit ('· 2.0R').
  Typing in the qty and price inputs re-plans and redraws only #posTradeDetail, so the cursor stays put; plan errors (too many, no price) show there and disable the send. closePlan(row, { type, qty, price, duration }): all of it = cancel every order in play that closes it, targets first and the nearest stop last; part of it = targets stay (qty capped at what limitsHolding leaves free) and the plain stops are cut to fit (cutStops, shared with targetPlan).
  Then a MARKET DAY close (SELL / BUY_TO_COVER / SELL_TO_CLOSE / BUY_TO_CLOSE), or a LIMIT paired with the nearest stop as OCO for the same qty (alone when there's no stop); a failed cancel means no close.
  Warned: a limit at or through the price (likely fills right away), Today with a pair.
  Confirmed when Schwab shows the position smaller by qty, or that many more shares in limits at the price.
  An order is in play unless FILLED / CANCELED / REJECTED / EXPIRED / REPLACED / AWAITING_PARENT_ORDER (so NEW, PENDING_*, QUEUED... count); restingOrders tags OCO legs with their pair.
  A cancel Schwab refuses is fine when a GET shows the order already gone (an OCO leg cancelled with its pair) and stops everything when it FILLED.
  No plan when the orders read failed (stopsMissing).
  At send, Schwab is re-read and the plan rebuilt with the same choices; if it errors or its steps differ from what was reviewed, nothing is sent and the sheet shows the new plan ('Schwab changed since you opened this…'); a warning that wasn't there at review (warnFlags: hours, fills right away, ...) also stops the send until it's been shown.
  Tickets carry the account hash: every step goes to that account and a switch mid-run stops it.
  Cut or replaced stops keep their type (STOP_LIMIT keeps its limit; a trailing stop can't be cut).
  Steps run in order, stop at the first failure, are sent once (ticket.sent), never retried; afterwards Schwab is re-read and the sheet says what it now shows ('Schwab now shows stops at $38.20 for all 300 shares', how many shares have no stop, a limit cancelled and not placed again).
  Unconfirmed results show every order Schwab lists on the symbol (ordersFor, any status) and block another send on that account and symbol for 2 minutes (state.posLast).
  Replace/cancel answers are assumed from the TD Ameritrade-era API (replace = cancel + new order, new id in Location); the re-read is the confirmation.
  Stop lasts uses schwab_stop_duration (schwabStopDuration).
  Warnings: outside regular hours, option stops/market orders, a same-symbol order in the last 2 minutes.

  **Working orders** (a second card under the table, from the same orders read; workingOrders in core/positions.js, pure, tested): every order still in play in the account, entries and orders on symbols you don't hold included (anything not FILLED / CANCELED / REJECTED / EXPIRED / REPLACED; AWAITING_PARENT_ORDER shows as 'waiting on entry'), one row per order with legs (children of TRIGGER and OCO orders walked), sorted by underlying, newest first, children right after their order.
  Columns: Symbol (option label), Order ('Sell stop', 'Buy to cover limit'; an OCO leg names its partner, 'one cancels the other with the stop $33.40', with a blue edge; a trigger child of an unfilled entry says 'after the buy fills'), Price (stop limit = stop over limit, trailing = trail over the current stop), Qty (remaining 'of N, M filled' on a partial), Lasts (Today / Until canceled, 'extended hours' when not NORMAL), Status, Placed (time today, else M/D; schwabTime adds the colon Safari needs to Schwab's +0000 offset).
  The pair note wraps so phones see symbol, order, price and qty before scrolling sideways.
  Orders read failed = 'Orders unavailable'.

  **Cancel** (ui/order-cancel.js, #cancelSheet = SHEETS.cancel, state.cancelTicket): every row with an id gets a Cancel button under the symbol, except an order waiting on its entry (AWAITING_PARENT_ORDER: it goes with the entry, so cancelPlan refuses it alone).
  It re-reads Schwab, then freezes cancelPlan (core/positions.js, pure): DELETE the order; Schwab cancels a one-cancels-other pair together, so for a pair leg the sheet asks Keep the stop / Keep the limit (the default: Schwab is asked about the other half first, read again a second apart for a few seconds while it's still on its way out, and it's placed again only when it shows CANCELED with no new fills; filled, partly filled, still working or unreadable stops the run and says what Schwab shows.
  Placed via singleOrder, same price, open qty, duration and session; STOP, LIMIT and STOP_LIMIT only, so a trailing partner greys it out) or Cancel both.
  Warnings: what a held position's stops still cover afterwards ('After this, stops cover 250 of your 300 HOOD' / 'have no stop'), shares without a stop until a kept stop is back, partial fills staying filled, an entry's waiting stop going with it.
  Sent once, nothing retries; a refused cancel is fine when a GET shows the order already gone and stops everything when it FILLED (the other half is never placed over a filled target).
  Confirmed when the re-read no longer lists it working and, when kept, shows a new order like the other half; unconfirmed lists what Schwab shows on the symbol and blocks the next send there for 2 minutes (state.posLast, shared with the position trades).

  **Edit** (ui/order-edit.js, #editSheet = SHEETS.edit, state.editTicket): beside Cancel on rows editable() allows (single-leg STOP, LIMIT, STOP_LIMIT, not waiting on an entry).
  It re-reads Schwab, then plans with editPlan (core/positions.js, pure): a form with the stop and/or limit (priceTick, options optionPriceTick), the size (the open quantity) and Today / Until canceled; typing re-plans from the same read and redraws only #editDetail.
  A plain order is one replace (PUT), keeping its session.
  A half of a pair can't be replaced alone, so the pair is cancelled and placed again as a new OCO with the change, the other half unchanged, both the same size and duration, and only once Schwab shows BOTH halves CANCELED with no new fills (shownCancelled, shared with Cancel; 'the changed pair was not placed' otherwise).
  A limit entry with one stop waiting on it (a Trade at Schwab limit that isn't filling) goes the same way: a replace would drop the stop, so the entry is cancelled (its stop goes with it) and placed again as a new TRIGGER with the change and the same stop, at the new size, only once Schwab shows the entry CANCELED with nothing filled ('the changed entry was not placed' otherwise).
  The form has its limit, size and Today / Until canceled (the stop keeps its own); the detail shows the risk at the stop if it fills at the limit, and a limit at or through the stop is blocked.
  A new limit whose risk at the stop is more than 10% over what the entry risked at its old limit is a warning (chasing a fill, or a fat finger).
  It's confirmed when the new entry shows with its stop waiting on it; a new entry without its stop says to check Schwab before it fills.
  Refused (error): an order waiting on its entry, an entry with anything else waiting on it (a market or stop entry, a target and a stop; a change would drop them), an entry already partly filled, trailing and multi-leg orders, a pair whose other half is trailing.
  Blocked in the form (editError): nothing changed, no price, a stop at or through the position's mark, more than you hold less the other closing orders (a pair counted once; only checked when the size goes up).
  A limit through the mark is a warning (likely fills right away).
  A refused replace or cancel stops the run, saying so when the order FILLED.
  At send, Schwab is re-read and the change re-planned; a changed order or steps sends nothing and shows the new plan.
  Confirmed when the re-read shows a new working order like the changed one (and its pair); unconfirmed blocks the symbol for 2 minutes like Cancel.
  Opening Cancel or Edit while that block is on says so and lists what Schwab shows on the symbol, as the position trades do.

  **Position chart + profit targets** (ui/position-chart.js, #posChartSection, state.posChart): every tradable row gets a **Chart** button first in the pinned column (Chart, Simulate on options, Stop, BE stop, Close, wrapping two by two on phones via .pos-acts.many); it toggles that position's charts (one open at a time, aria-pressed on the button).
  Two panes, like the calculator and the Journal's trade page: **5 min** (#posCol5, #posChart5) and **Daily** (#posColD, #posChartD), each state.posChart.five / .daily with its own bars, view, Today and hover (and on the daily, a range chip).
  Both are read from Tradier at once when the chart opens, on tradierSymbol(symbol), so an option's are the contract's own: fetchFiveMinuteBars (the last two weeks, mapped to { date, time, open, high, low, close, volume }, then cut to the latest session: latestSession; a thin contract has only the 5-minute windows it traded in; no answer = 'needs a brokerage-linked key') and dailyHistory.
  A pane without bars hides and #posChartStatus says why (loading, or what Tradier answered); the other pane takes the card, and with neither the plot hides.
  Side by side (5 min left, 3:2, like the trade page) once the card itself is 600px wide (about a 1200px window) (a container query on .pos-chart, since on the desktop shell it shares the row with the table), stacked with 5 min on top below that.
  The 5-minute pane is today's session only (owner request), no range chips: before the open, on a weekend or for a contract that didn't trade today it is the last day that traded, and the caption says which ('5 min today', or '5 min Fri 10/24': sessionWords, New York's date).
  It has time ticks, a '5m M/D HH:MM' readout (candles.js reads bar.time) with the bar's VWAP, and Latest → (#posChartToday5) when zoomed and panned off the newest bars.
  Its indicators: the session VWAP (teal, sessionVwap from core/trade-page.js) and the prior day's high, low and close (PDH / PDL / PDC: priorDay, the daily bar before the 5-minute chart's latest session; faint dotted lines labelled left, drawn only when they fall inside the scale and never stretching it).
  The daily pane gets 1M / 3M / 6M / 1Y chips (starting at the daily_range preference).
  The daily's chips call setPosChartRange(count), Today carries the pane in data-arg (posChartToday(pane)); one readout (#posChartOhlc) shows whichever pane is hovered.
  The card sits under the table on phones and tablets (scrolled into view) and BESIDE it on the desktop shell (#positionsSplit gets .open → a two-column grid; below 1100 it is display:contents and the card is order 12 after the table).
  Bars come from Tradier, fetched once per open (no key = a note and no chart, the target can still be typed); the views, range chips (not saved) and Today chips are the panes' own, separate from the calculator's charts.
  Drawn on both panes: candles + 8 EMA, avg cost (blue, labelled left; the **Avg** chip in the card's head, beside ×, hides it and drops it from the price scale, so a cost far from the price doesn't squeeze the candles; state.posChartAvg, pos_chart_avg, device-local, on by default; the card's meta line still says avg), every working stop (red) and limit target (green) with sizes added per price (positionLevels), and the target being set (solid green).
  Tap/click (or long-press release) on either plot sets the price of the tool picked in the form's switch (Profit target | Stop | Move breakeven, pickTarget), snapped to a candle's exact high or low within 6px of it (snapToBar; hover snaps the same, readout shows the price and % from now); the date axis isn't a price.
  Form: Target price (priceTick: nearest cent, 4 decimals under $1; options optionPriceTick, the $0.05 / $0.10 grid, priced on the option itself, not the underlying), share or contract count, portion chips 10% ¼ ⅓ ½ All (of the position, at least 1, capped at what other limits leave: targetRoom; All = that rest), a live summary from the same pure plan (gain over cost, %, R off the nearest stop, the stop it pairs with, or the error that blocks it), and **Review target** → openPositionTrade(symbol, 'target'), which freezes price and qty from the form, re-reads Schwab and plans from that. targetPlan (core/positions.js): a LIMIT closing qty at the target (SELL / BUY_TO_COVER, SELL_TO_CLOSE / BUY_TO_CLOSE for options, Orders last = schwab_stop_duration); when the position has any stop it goes in as OCO(limit, stop at the NEAREST stop's price, same qty, same duration) so the target's shares keep a stop, and the plain stops are cut to the shares left outside every limit (walk nearest first; the farthest are cancelled, the boundary one replaced with fewer shares at its own price and duration: 'Cut stop $33.40 from 300 shares to 200'; plan.moved = shares without a stop until the pair lands, said in the hint).
  No stop = the limit alone.
  **Target in R** (#posTargetR, R chips 1R 2R 3R 5R in #posTargetRChips, posChart.r): sets the target price that many R from your average cost (above on a long, below on a short), on the same grid (rTargetPrice in core/positions.js, pure), so 'sell 10% at 5R' is the 5R chip and the 10% chip.
  The R chips are your own: ✎ at the end of the row turns them into number boxes (blank deletes one, the trailing '+' box adds one) and itself into ✓, which saves the positive ones, deduped, smallest first, at most 5 (rPresets / rChipRow / rEditToggled in ui/position-chart.js, state.rEdit while editing; r_presets in localStorage, default 1 2 3 5, not synced).
  The chart's Target in R and the close ticket's Limit in R share them, and editing either redraws both.
  1R is rUnit (core/positions.js, pure): the risk the R column counts from, per share or contract, so the Journal trade's risk at entry over its entry size when it matches (trims don't change it), else the nearest stop's distance from your cost, or a long option's premium with no stop (positionRUnit in ui/positions.js; an open chart reads the Journal once like the R column does, signed in).
  Typing a price or tapping the chart clears the R and the box shows the price in R; the summary says what 1R is and where it's from ('1R is $2.70 a share, from the stop $35.50'), and the review's At target counts R the same way.
  No risk to count from (no stop on a stock, a stop past breakeven, no cost basis): the chips grey out with why, and a typed R says so and blocks the review.
  Hovering on Profit target adds the price in R to the readout ('· 2.6R').
  Blocked: fractional shares, a target at or through the price, qty over what other limits leave, and the same odd orders breakeven refuses (limitsHolding, shared with breakevenPlan).
  Warnings: outside regular hours, and Today with a pair (the paired stop ends with the target at the close).
  Confirmed when Schwab's re-read shows qty more shares in LIMIT closers at the target price; then the form clears and the chart draws it from Schwab's orders.
  **Stop** (the switch's middle tool, #posStopForm: #posChartStopPrice, #posStopSummary, #posStopReview, state.posChart.stop): the tapped or typed stop is drawn solid red ('new stop'), the summary checks it with stopPlan (any of the three cover choices working is enough) and says what every share at it risks from cost with % of the account, past the max loss per trade, and when a limit means the review will ask what it covers.
  **Review stop** → openPositionTrade(symbol, 'stop') with the chart's price in the input; once Schwab shows it, positionStopPlaced clears the form.
  Each 30s read re-renders the card (lines, chips, summary) without touching the inputs; account changes close it.

  **Move breakeven** (the switch's third tool, state.posChartTool, kept while charts open and close; a calculator, nothing goes to Schwab): tap the chart where you want your stop (same snapping, priceTick / optionPriceTick) and it says how many shares or contracts to add so your average cost lands on it, so that stop risks nothing.
  breakevenAdd (core/positions.js, pure): qty = held × (stop − avg) ÷ (add price − stop), rounded down, so the new average stops just short of the stop and the stop locks a little (shown); the add price is the mark unless one is typed (Add at).
  The stop has to sit between your cost and the add price (long: above the cost, under the price; short: the other way); otherwise, or under 1 share, the form says why.
  The summary: what to buy (or short) and what it costs, the new size and average, the position's value and % of account at that price, what your stops risk now, and to move the stop for every share once it fills; fees aren't counted.
  The chart draws the stop (solid red, 'BE stop' with the new size) and a typed add price (solid blue, with the add size), and hovering says how many to add for a stop right there ('· add 490').
